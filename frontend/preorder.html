Pre-Order Inquiries

Everything you need to know regarding Batch 01 reservations and cellar dispatch.

What makes Batch 01 special?
Batch 01 represents the virgin extraction and cellar bottling of House of Aavasa. Capped strictly at 150 flacons per formulation, each bottle receives hand-poured attention, slow 28-day cold maceration, complimentary laser monogramming, and an archival wax-sealed authenticity certificate.
When will my pre-order be delivered?
Maceration concludes on Day 28 (late October 2026). Bottles will undergo micro-filtration, hand-bottling, and collar engraving, before shipping via 48-hour priority air courier across India. You will receive active WhatsApp and SMS tracking the moment your flacon departs the cellar.
Can I order with Cash on Delivery (Pay on Delivery)?
Yes. You may reserve your numbered flacon today with zero advance payment. You simply pay upon courier delivery at your doorstep when the matured bottle arrives in late October.
How does the complimentary bottle engraving work?
When reserving your flacon, you can input up to 3 initials (e.g. "R S M"). Our master engraver laser-etches these initials directly onto the mirror-polished gold flacon collar. If left blank, your flacon collar will feature the classic Aavasa seal.
Can I cancel or modify my reservation?
Absolutely. Reservations can be updated or cancelled at any time up until 48 hours prior to bottling dispatch by emailing concierge@houseofaavasa.com or messaging our concierge line.
