Frequently Asked

Pre-Order Inquiries

Key information regarding Batch 01 reservations and dispatch.

What makes Batch 01 special?
Batch 01 represents the virgin extraction and cellar bottling of House of Aavasa. Each bottle receives hand-poured attention, slow cold maceration, complimentary laser monogramming, and an archival wax-sealed authenticity certificate.
When will my pre-order be delivered?
Bottles will undergo micro-filtration, hand-bottling, and collar engraving, before shipping via express insured courier across India in late October 2026. You will receive active tracking the moment your flacon departs our atelier.
What payment methods are accepted for Batch 01 Pre-Orders?
Because Batch 01 represents a private, custom laser-monogrammed allocation, all pre-orders are 100% prepaid online. We securely accept Instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Debit & Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), and NetBanking via Razorpay. Cash on Delivery is not available for custom pre-order allocations.
How does the complimentary bottle engraving work?
When reserving your flacon, you can input up to 3 initials (e.g. "R S M"). Our master engraver laser-etches these initials directly onto the mirror-polished gold flacon collar. If left blank, your flacon collar will feature the classic Aavasa seal.
Can I cancel or modify my reservation?
Yes. Reservations can be updated or cancelled at any time up until dispatch by contacting concierge@houseofaavasa.com.
